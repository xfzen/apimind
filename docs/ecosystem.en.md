# Ecosystem and Commercial Boundary

[中文](ecosystem.md)

Independent clients may be open or closed source, free or paid. Selling a client alone does not automatically trigger Server commercial licensing; the Server's actual use determines whether commercial rights are required.

Personal noncommercial persistent self-hosting may be free under the Server BUSL Additional Use Grant. Organization evaluation, development, testing, internal CI, and PoC are free. Every organization's production use, including nonprofit and academic production, requires commercial rights unless a separately reviewed exception is granted.

Commercial Server rights are required for:

- enterprise or other organization production deployment;
- bundling, customer delivery, or private deployment projects;
- managed hosting or SaaS;
- OEM, embedding, or white-label use;
- charging third parties for capabilities based on Server.

The [Server licensing guide](https://github.com/xfzen/apimind-server/blob/dev/LICENSING.md) and any signed commercial agreement control the final boundary.
